# JANSETU AI — PRODUCT REQUIREMENTS DOCUMENT

## Vision
JANSETU AI converts fragmented citizen development requests into evidence-backed infrastructure priorities.

## Problem
Citizen requests arrive through voice, messaging, web, meetings, and grievance channels. They are fragmented, multilingual, duplicated, difficult to compare, and poorly connected to infrastructure and investment data.

## Goals
- consolidate citizen demand
- support multilingual participation
- detect duplicate/similar issues
- identify geographic hotspots
- combine demand with demographics, infrastructure and investment data
- produce transparent priority scores
- recommend candidate projects
- track human decisions
- measure impact
- preserve citizen trust and privacy

## Users
Citizen; Government Analyst; Policymaker; Program Manager; Administrator.

## MVP Features

### Citizen
- language selection
- voice submission
- text submission
- optional photo
- location
- AI confirmation
- request status
- project/impact updates

### Government
- dashboard
- hotspot map
- cluster explorer
- priority queue
- evidence panel
- policy copilot
- budget simulator
- policy brief generator
- impact dashboard

## Core User Story
Citizen speaks in Gujarati about a monsoon road problem → Gemini understands it → similar requests are clustered → infrastructure/demographic/investment datasets are joined → deterministic priority score is calculated → a candidate project is generated → policymaker reviews evidence → project impact is tracked.

## North Star Metric
Citizen-to-Action Conversion Rate =
high-priority demands converted to validated development actions / total high-priority demands

## Differentiator
JANSETU is not a complaint chatbot. It is a public infrastructure demand intelligence layer connecting citizen demand to evidence, prioritization, investment planning and measurable outcomes.

## BRICS Strategy
Build India-first demo; country-specific configuration supports languages, administrative hierarchy, currency, datasets and policy weights for future BRICS deployments.

## Non-goals
No automatic funding approval, no individual profiling, no fabricated live data, no replacement of government authority.
